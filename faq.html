Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about shopping with URBAN FIT.

Got questions?

Find answers to our most commonly asked questions below.

GENERAL

What is URBAN FIT?
URBAN FIT is a men's fashion brand focused on timeless essentials, versatile clothing and everyday style.
How can I contact customer support?
Visit our Contact Us page to find our contact information.

ORDERS & PAYMENTS

How do I place an order?
Browse our Shop, select a product, choose your size and quantity, and add it to your bag. Follow the checkout steps when online ordering is enabled.
Which payment methods are accepted?
Available payment methods will be displayed at checkout. Online payments are not yet connected in this demo website.
Can I cancel or change my order?
Please contact customer support as soon as possible. Changes and cancellations depend on the order status and the store's final order policy.

SHIPPING & RETURNS

How long does delivery take?
Delivery timelines will depend on your location and the shipping service. The confirmed estimate should be shown when placing an actual order.
Can I return or exchange a product?
Please check the store's final return and exchange policy before placing an order. Contact customer support for help.
How can I track my order?
Order tracking can be provided once order processing and shipping services are connected to the website.

PRODUCTS & SIZING

How do I choose the right size?
Check the size options available on each product page. A detailed size chart can be added to help you choose the right fit.
Will sold-out products be restocked?
Restock availability depends on the product. Contact us to enquire about a specific item.
