Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Learn how URBAN FIT handles information when you browse our website.

Last updated: October 20, 2026
01

Introduction

Welcome to URBAN FIT. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or interact with our website.

By using our website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please avoid submitting personal information through the website.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Contact details, such as your name and email address, if you provide them.
  • Delivery and billing details if an ordering feature is introduced.
  • Messages or feedback you send through our contact forms.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, if collected by website tools.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and the website specifically requests it through an appropriate process.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and customer requests.
  • Improve website content, design and shopping experience.
  • Provide requested updates where you have agreed to receive them.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.
04

Cookies and Website Technologies

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use their pages.

Our demonstration cart uses your browser's local storage to remember cart items on your device. This is separate from a secure online order or payment system.

Analytics or advertising cookies should only be described as active if those tools are actually installed and used on the live website.

05

Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information as part of the website experience. Information may need to be shared with service providers if features such as hosting, customer support, delivery or payments are introduced.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law. Actual sharing practices depend on the services configured for the live website.

06

Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. However, no method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for its intended purpose or as required by law.

07

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. You may also be able to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

You can contact us through our Contact page to make a privacy-related request.

08

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We do not control their privacy practices, content or security. Please review their policies before providing personal information on those websites.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without the permissions required by applicable law.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website or services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

HAVE A QUESTION?

Your privacy is important.

Contact us if you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled.
